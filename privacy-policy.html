Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026 | Learn how MTR Global collects, protects, and handles your personal information.

1. Introduction

Welcome to MTR Global. We are committed to protecting your personal privacy and maintaining the security of any personal information received from you. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or utilize our overseas recruitment services.

2. Information We Collect

To provide manpower recruitment and deployment services, we may collect personal details, including but not limited to:

  • Personal Details: Full name, age, gender, date of birth, and nationality.
  • Contact Information: Phone number, email address, and residential address.
  • Identification & Travel Documents: Passport details, National ID, GAMCA medical clearance, and trade test certificates.
  • Professional Details: Resume/CV, work history, skill certifications, and educational qualification.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly for legitimate business and recruitment purposes, such as:

  • Matching candidate profiles with overseas employer requirements.
  • Processing visa applications, work permits, and embassy attestations.
  • Flight ticketing, GAMCA medical coordination, and deployment briefing.
  • Communicating application status and recruitment updates.

4. Sharing Your Information

We respect your data privacy. We do not sell or trade your personal information. However, we may share relevant details with trusted third parties solely for processing your deployment:

  • Verified International Employers seeking manpower.
  • Government Embassies, Consulates, and Immigration Departments for Visa Stamping.
  • Authorized Medical Centers (GAMCA) and Airlines for ticketing.

5. Data Security

We implement strict administrative, technical, and physical security measures to safeguard your personal data from unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. All candidate records are stored in secure, encrypted databases.
