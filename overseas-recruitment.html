Our 6-Step Recruitment Workflow

01

Demand & Requirement Sourcing

We analyze the employer's Demand Letter (DL), Power of Attorney (POA), and specific Job Descriptions.

02

Sourcing & Candidate Shortlisting

Tapping into our extensive database and conducting regional recruitment drives to shortlist top matches.

03

Trade Testing & Interviews

Practical skills evaluation at government-approved trade test centers and face-to-face/virtual client interviews.

04

Medical Checkup & Documentation

GAMCA/Approved medical checkups, PCC (Police Clearance Certificate) verification, and background checks.

05

Visa Stamping & Flight Deployment

Embassy processing, work permit approval, flight ticketing, and pre-departure orientation briefing (PDOB).
