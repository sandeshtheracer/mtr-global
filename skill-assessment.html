Ensuring Right Skills for High-Impact Roles

To eliminate deployment risks for international clients, MTR Global conducts rigorous practical and theoretical trade testing. We partner with government-accredited polytechnic centers and technical trade test institutes to evaluate every worker before shortlisting.

From precision welding (6G / TIG / MIG) and heavy equipment operation to electrical wiring and healthcare clinical rounds, our testing guarantees that deployed candidates are 100% job-ready from Day 1.
